## Why Skollmunity?

Many see low-level computer science and visual arts as opposites. To me, they are two sides of the same coin: **understanding how underlying systems work**.

In systems programming, you dissect memory layouts, instruction sets, and execution flow. In visual theory, you dissect light wavelengths, neurological perception, and cultural semiotics. Color is not merely aesthetic decoration—it is an optimized communication protocol between an artist's vision and the human subconscious.

**Skollmunity** was born out of this curiosity:
- **How do superhero costumes convey morality?** We analyze primary vs. secondary color wheels in character design.
- **How do filmmakers build dread or ambition?** We break down palettes in works like *Whiplash*, *Squid Game*, and *Gurren Lagann*.
- **Why do specific hues evoke calm or danger?** We explore the psychology of red, blue, yellow, green, gold, and silver.

Originally published on WordPress, this entire platform is now **100% open source** and statically compiled with **Jekyll on GitHub Pages**.

## The Skoll Ecosystem

Skollmunity is part of a larger ecosystem of open source software and visual experiments:

- **🌐 [Skoll Portfolio](https://skollwarynz.github.io)**: Main hub for software, security research, and visual analysis.
- **🎲 [SkollDice](https://skollwarynz.github.io/SkollDice/)**: A deterministic, high-performance RPG dice engine written from scratch in bare-metal C.
- **🏰 [SkollCTF (Baki Tower)](https://codeberg.org/Skollwarynz)**: Interactive binary exploitation track covering stack overflows, ROP chains, and shellcoding.
- **📄 [Formal Verification Research](https://skollwarynz.github.io)**: UniTo thesis on *Probabilistic Model Checking with Symmetry Reduction* (evaluating PRISM &amp; GRIP efficiency).
