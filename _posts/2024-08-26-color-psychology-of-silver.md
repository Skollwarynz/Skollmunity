## A long history

Similarly to [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">gold</span>,](/gold-psychology/) the history behind this precious material is really long and deeply connected with the material itself. Silver was largely used in different societies in the past, like

- Chinese
- Greek
- Romans
- Egyptian

In Chinese culture, it is used as a symbol of purity and success. As in other civilizations, its main use was to produce jewelry.

The idea of success and luxury was common in Greek and Roman society too, because of the wide use of silver to create money besides jewels.

In Greek mythology, statues were even used as decorations and symbols of the goddess Artemis, the goddess of the moon. The associations of femininity and the moon are connected with silver even now.

Instead, Egyptians believed in the divinity of silver. They thought that silver was the god's bone material.

## Purity and folklore elements

The idea of purity (similar to that of white, as we already talked about this color [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">here</span>](/white/)) is deeply connected with the symbolism of epic monsters.

Usually, in stories about monsters (usually used to represent the corruption of man), the hero uses a silver weapon to defeat these monsters.

For example, werewolves (monsters that appear even in Greek mythology) are weak against the purity of this material.

In addition, in some teenage series, Silver appears to work on vampires and other common monsters of folklore too.

![werewolf halloween statue](/assets/images/2024/08/werewolf-statue.jpg)

## Silver, symbol of branding

The perfect balance between [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">black</span>](/color-black/) and [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">white</span>](/white/) gives this hue the idea of balance. In addition to the balance, the colors white and black mix and produce the idea of impartiality and impersonality.

For this reason, the idea of impersonality often connects with branding. In fact, most businesses try to use this color or similar shades to attract as many people as possible. In the future, we'll talk more about the color used in branding, but the main idea is that the use of a more vibrant color can create a stronger identity.

![Silver statue of Justice](/assets/images/2024/08/silver-libra-320x480.jpg)

## Silver Film and Sci-fi

On the other hand, the power of reflection and impersonality is often used in the film industry to create particular effects, especially in sci-fi movies. The ideas of impersonality and balance are reshaped into objects, like robots. The world of robots, spaceships, and futuristic technology connects deeply with the shades of silver and gray.
