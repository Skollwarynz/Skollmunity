If this is the first article about color psychology that you read, I suggest you start your journey from the introduction (click [here](/color-psychology/) to go there). The color black is a strange hue because it is the absence of color itself. Even in this case our brain has some reaction:

### **Positive one:**

- Authority
- Elegance
- Formality
- Intelligence
- Power
- Prestige

### **Negative one:**

- death
- sad
- edgy
- anger
- aggression
- fear
- mystery
- sadness

As usual we'll try to cover all these different effects even if some of them are deeply connected with other chromatic hues (anger is connected with [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">red</span>](/color-red-psychological-effects/), as is sadness, which is connected with [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">blue</span>](/color-blue/)).

## **The color black is a sign of power, elegance, and more**

The stereotypical clothing code is tied to the majority of Black's positive connotations. 

The black suit is a representation of elegance, much like the red tie that conveyed the impression of authority (we discussed the influence of red [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">here</span>](/color-red-psychological-effects/)). 

The image of elegance links to the idea of the successful man, making it easier to picture a smart, strong individual.

Wearing black is also associated with formality, prestige, and even authority

[![Black and white image of a man drinking whiskey.](/assets/images/2023/07/man-in-a-black-suit.jpg)](/assets/images/2023/07/man-in-a-black-suit.jpg)

## **Color black, a symbol of death**

The representation of death usually uses dark hues. The representation of death and loss produces a direct connection with the 5 phases of grief:

- Denial
- Anger
- Bargaining
- Depression
- Acceptance

Anger and sadness are connected with dark tones of, respectively, red and blue.

[![Image of a woman with a body-painted skeleton.](/assets/images/2023/07/black-symbol-of-death.jpg)](/assets/images/2023/07/black-symbol-of-death.jpg)

## **Color of mystery and fear**

The black hue is the color that dominates the nighttime. As we said in the article about the color [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">blue</span>,](/color-blue/) our minds associate the color of nature with specific perceptions. Black is associated with nighttime and so with mystery because we can't see in the dark, and with that there is a direct connection with fear.

The film industry uses this hue to create mysterious characters. For this reason the dark colors are also a symbol of badness and evilness. Even comics used these effects to create some mysterious heroes, like, for example, [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">Batman</span>](/category/superheroes).
