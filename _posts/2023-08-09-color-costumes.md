Before starting our explanation of colour theory, I want you to know that we'll talk about the design of American heroes, so the rules applied can be different if we consider other cultures and graphic representation.

## **Primary color in costumes**

Normally, the [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">primary colours</span>](/color-theory2-color-wheel/) ([<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">red</span>](/color-red-psychological-effects/), [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">blue</span>](/color-blue/), and [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">yellow</span>](/effect-of-yellow-on-our-mind/)) are used in the representation of the main heroes. The normal representation is based on the use of mostly two colours.

### **Red and blue**

We can take, as an example, the [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">Spider-Man</span>](https://spiderman.fandom.com/wiki/Spider-Man_Wiki) suit: blue and red are the two main hues of the costume.

The red means they are bold, passionate, and determined but grounded with the blue's depth, wisdom, and confidence.

Clearly, this effect has a different balance depending on how the hero is described during the story. The importance of colour has the power to advise the reader about everything except the hero before they know it. The use of this combination of red and blue is particularly effective:

- [Spider-man](https://spiderman.fandom.com/wiki/Spider-Man_Wiki)
- [Captain America](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Steven_Rogers_(Earth-616))
- [Thor](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Thor_Odinson_(Earth-616))
- [Wonder Woman](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Wonder_Woman_(Diana_Prince))
- [Superman](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Superman_(Clark_Kent))

In the last two examples, the colour yellow helps to inspire joy and energy too, creating a hero that brings peace and joy, a symbol of peace (like Batman).

[![superhero](/assets/images/2023/08/Superhero-960x960.jpg)](http://www.freepik.com/)

### **Red and Yellow**

[Red](/color-red-psychological-effects/) and [yellow](/effect-of-yellow-on-our-mind/) are another common combination usually used in iconic costumes.

They blend vibrant reds' exhilarating burning rage with yellow's vivacity and flashy character. 

They frequently portray cockier, swaggering heroes. 

The red and yellow combine to form a dancing fire, while the yellow indicates that safety has arrived.

As before, a lot of heroes used these shades:

- [Iron Man](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Iron_Man)
- [Flash](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Flash)
- [Shazam](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Shazam_(Billy_Batson))
- [Plastic Man](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Patrick_O'Brian_(New_Earth))
- [Robin](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Robin)

In the case of Robin, the presence of green suggests the playful spirit of a boy and helps to understand the youth of this hero.

[![red and yellow superhero costume](/assets/images/2023/08/red-and-yellow-superhero-960x960.jpg)](http://www.freepik.com/)

### **Yellow and blue**

The last primary colour pair, blue and yellow, is used for characters that are a little out of the ordinary. 

They lack the scarlet aggressiveness of those who aim for or are predestined for the foreground.

The blue represents wisdom, and the yellow represents joy and energy.

Thanos, then, is a little exception to this rule. The blue and yellow can remind us of wisdom, but the purple of the skin, which is a secondary colour (we'll talk about it in the next article), suggests the role of an antagonist.

A lot of heroes use this colour. Here are some examples:

- The old [X-men](https://x-men.fandom.com/wiki/X-Men_Wiki) costumes
- [Nova](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Nova) (in this case, the costumes imitate a cop's uniform)
- [Doctor Fate](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Kent_Nelson_(New_Earth))
