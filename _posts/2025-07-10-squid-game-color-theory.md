## Introduction

The series Squid Game produced a lot of hype around the third and final season. After 3 different seasons, I'm here to talk about an important aspect of the series: colors. Here we start a new category of blog called "TV series and films." Here we'll talk about color theory and color psychology in films and TV series. The category collects various posts about specific topics and is easy to find through the section on the menu. But now let's start with the main topic.

The color theory of Squid Game is represented by 3 different main colors: cyan, magenta, and [yellow](/effect-of-yellow-on-our-mind/). We'll briefly recollect some common symbolism of these hues, but I linked in the different colors to the pages about the complete article on their color psychology.

## Cyan

Cyan is a color with a lot of common effects of [blue](/color-blue/), like calmness; at the same time, the calmness is a calculated one, more like a symbol of survival and adaptation. These themes became central in the series.

## Magenta

Magenta is a special color. For now the important aspect is that magenta resembles [red.](/color-red-psychological-effects/) This similarity will be used to represent danger, blood, and more importantly, the guards of the games.

## Yellow

And in the end we have yellow, the last primary color on our list. This hue is complex and has different meanings; however, in this context, it is used to represent wealth and power, like the money pot, the true objective of the players. Often it resembles the color of [gold](/gold-psychology/) and usually is used to represent it.

## The hierarchy in Squid Game

The main colors were cited before. All the series follow an internal hierarchy dictated by the color used. We can divide the cast into three main categories:

- People
- Money
- System

The theory is based on the definition of people with the color cyan, money represented with the color yellow, and finally the system represented by the color magenta.

[![Gemini generated image f1yvg4f1yvg4f1yv 960x960](/assets/images/2025/07/Gemini_Generated_Image_f1yvg4f1yvg4f1yv-960x960.png)](/assets/images/2025/07/Gemini_Generated_Image_f1yvg4f1yvg4f1yv-960x960.png)

The theory continues with the implication of the fusion of different colors. Although primary colors deflect different patterns (money, people, and system), their interactions produce more colors.

## Teal

The color teal represents the players, which is the fusion of cyan and yellow.

So, the fusion of these two colors represents the people (cyan) who follow money (yellow), the true objective of Squid Game.

The color teal add more symbolism because it represents vulnerability and disposability

[![Gemini generated image glncfhglncfhglnc 960x960](/assets/images/2025/07/Gemini_Generated_Image_glncfhglncfhglnc-960x960.png)](/assets/images/2025/07/Gemini_Generated_Image_glncfhglncfhglnc-960x960.png)

## Blue

The color [blue](/color-blue/) represents the people (cyan) who followed a system (magenta), like the police officers displayed during the series.

These characters are not as central in the plot as the player or the guards, but they play a crucial role in dividing the player from normal life.

In addition, the blue guards are the guards in everyday life and become unconsciously part of the system on which the Squid Game is based.

[![Gemini generated image ajdyynajdyynajdy 960x960](/assets/images/2025/07/Gemini_Generated_Image_ajdyynajdyynajdy-960x960.png)](/assets/images/2025/07/Gemini_Generated_Image_ajdyynajdyynajdy-960x960.png)

## Red

The color [red](/color-red-psychological-effects/) represents danger and alert. In this case, it is presented when the system (magenta) follows money (yellow), so the fusion produces the red of blood.

The idea is present in the series too; when the avarice of the guards became too much, they would be eliminated from the system because of their pursuit of money over their original job.

Another layer of meaning is added because in Korean culture the color red represents wealth and power and is used to underline the power of the system against humans.

## Black

[Black](/color-black/) in the subtractive color theory is the fusion of all colors together.

In this case it represents a person (cyan) who's part of the system (magenta) and at the same time follows or has money (yellow), and that person is represented by the Frontman.

The only person aside from the VIPs who has power and wealth.

*[Image: Yes 960x960]*

In the end are presented interactions of different color palettes or single hues that produce different meanings throughout the series.

## Gold

This is an extra section of the theory because, as I said before, gold is often represented with yellow and usually is used as a substitute for it.

But actually the color [gold](/gold-psychology/) has a deeper meaning aside from the simple representation of money.

The color gold is used as a symbol of VIPs through the masks they wear. In this case the color gold represents wealth and power because in these games the only thing that really matters is money. So the gold is used to increment the division between the weak and fragile teal player and the power and indifference of the wealthy that pay to watch the show.

## Pink

The color pink is used as a secondary color in the series. Although it usually provokes calmness and love in the series, it became the main contradiction of the series. Indeed, the presence of a childish game and a childish environment is used to create contrast from the real nature of the Squid Game.

## Dualism: red and blue

The first unofficial game of Squid Game is the ddakji.

If you pay attention, the presence of blue and red is already presented here. In this case the colors represent an old Japanese legend. The legend talks about a spirit who offers red or blue paper and, in either choice, ends the life of the unfortunate. This hints at the destiny reserved for all the players during the game. In addition, the legend says that if you decide to decline the offer, you'll be safe as well as the player, and that if you don't accept, we'll probably live. In conclusion, the legend gains an additional layer of irony, as the recruiter selects individuals already deemed 'dead' by society, rendering an unfavorable outcome inevitable for the player, regardless of their choice.

## Dualism: Magenta and teal

The color chosen to represent the player (teal) and the guards of the game (magenta) is not casual.

The two colors chosen are complete opposites inside the [color wheel,](/color-theory2-color-wheel/) and that's used to create more differentiation on the screen and emphasize the opposition of the two roles. On the one hand we have teal, a symbol of survival; on the other we have magenta, a color that resembles red and his violence and danger.
