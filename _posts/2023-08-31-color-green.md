## **Green,color of nature and fertility**

The color green is a short-wavelength hue, in color psychology, tints with a short wavelength are considered relazing or cool hues.The chilly tones like blue (we talked about this color [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">here</span>](/color-blue/)) or green impact our stress level and creativity.In fact, studies show that rooms painted with these hues can increase the creativity of the people who work inside them.

The effect of relaxation is strengthened by the connection between green and nature. We aready talkd about this correaltion in the last article about the use of secondary color on superhero costumes (click [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">here</span>](/superhero-costume-design-secondary-color/) to read it).

The ancient cultures connected the idea of nature with the idea of fertility.This correlation caused green to become a symbol of those aspects too.

## **Color of optimism?**

Even the most cheerful color hide his own dark side. Althought studies shows that a green enviroment is associated with a positive thinking, the darkest hues are generally symbols of negative attitudes. Greed and envy hide in the darkest tint of green. Even disgust for the correlation with vegetables like broccoli. In the film "[<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">Inside out</span>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inside_Out_(2015_film))" the character that represent disgust is green. In addition green is saw as a factor of sickness.

## **Green and bad luck in hystory**

Actors sometimes view the color green as unfortunate since the French playwright and actor Molière died while wearing a green costume. 

Even so, there may have been a valid reason to look green darkly in Victorian times due to the usage of arsenic in popular green fabrics and wallpaper, which resulted in several unhappy deaths.

So the connection between green and bad luck have a really long story.
