## **Color orange: symbol of joy and energy**

Orange is a strong color that combines the strength of yellow and red. 

Yellow's stimulating effects are combined with red's powerful emotional responses.

Orange is typically thought of as a cheerful and bright hue. 

The industry uses this powerful response at the same time to develop brands that command attention.

However the intense reaction is the primary reason for the intense love and hatred associated with this hue. 

A lot of people don't really like it because although some think it to be a great color for his jovial and energizing temperament, others find it to be too intense.

## **Autmn and Halloween**

Instinct is one of the primary reasons why color affects our thoughts and emotions, as we already stated in the article on color psychology (click here to [read](/color-psychology/) it).

Orange is associated with autumn because of how much the seasons affect our perception. 

The warmth of this color also link the drop in temperature during the change in season.

The prominence of orange throughout the Halloween season due to pumpkins further strengthens the direct association between orange and autumn.
