Art still extist?

I keep hearing that contemporary artwork isn't creative expression as I learn more about it. 

Art was only produced by the traditional painter and sculptur. We're here, as usual, to have a nice calm discussion on this subject.

## **What was art in past?**

Generally speaking, when I hear about "artistic expression," people usually allude to the greatest sculptors or painters of history.

Try to understand what we mean by stating that the "good old art" is the first issue.

## **A little bit of history**

We can start with the beginning of real mastercpiecesncluding such masterpieces of Michelangelo, Raffaello, or Leonardo. At that time, people utilized painting as a decor for churches or as a method of attempting to imitate the reality.

Then we had the period of the Neoclassicism where the artist tried to create sculpturs and painting as Greek and Latins use to do. The main themes were rationality and calm. After that we had Romaticism were nature and emotion were the main themes.
