### **Warm color in nature**

![Image of a street in a little wood. The season is autumn to express how color temperature influence the perception of image.](/assets/images/2023/07/autumn_image.jpg)

The foundation of our understanding of color temperature begins with instinct: in nature, warm colors serve as indicators of seasoning. 

After the summer's hot sun, the colors of autumn indicate a drop in temperature.

During times, the brand industry appeals to this aspect of our instinct and employs an array of warm colors.For instance, businesses utilize orange as their primary color during Halloween, whereas red acts as their main colour during Christmas.

### **Warm color effect on our body and mood**

The warm hue have an impact on our bodies. In fact,according to certain research,  people's appetites increase by warmer colors. This is why many fast food or restaurant logos and interior designs include these colors.

![Hamburger with bacon and cheese](/assets/images/2023/07/red-hamburger.jpg)

Warm colors can also make individuals feel closer to one another and alter their unconscious spatial perception.

### **Cool color in nature**

The temperature that we experience is impacted by the hue that surround us. 

For instance, the clothing industry uses cooler colors during the summer because they convey a feeling of colder temperatures while also absorbing less heat than darker hues.

### **Warm color effect on our body and mood**

Cool colors, especially blue and green, are useful to stimulate our brain.In fact cool color are used in schools to stimulate a more creative thinking or to build a peaceful enviroment. At the same time the cool color have different menaning that we'll encouter in future article.
