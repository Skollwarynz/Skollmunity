In this post, we'll start a discussion regarding how the colour red might influence our subconscious minds.

## Different association

Red is one of the most controversial colours since it is connected to so many different aspects of the mind:

- Love and romance
- Gentle
- Warmth and comfort
- Excitement and energy
- Blood
- Power
- Danger
- Lust
- Embarrassment

## **Red = love.**

When we think of red, love is generally the association that comes to mind first.

It has been demonstrated that this colour raises blood pressure, alters metabolism, and quickens heart and breathing rates.

The strong bodily connection between this colour and the emotion of love is a result of its significant physical effects.

![Image of a couple kissing and hugging, with both of them wearing red clothes.](/assets/images/2023/07/couple-in-love-960x1440.jpg)

## **Excitement**

As said before, studies show that this peculiar hue can affect our body:

- Elevated blood pressure
- Enhanced metabolism
- Increased heart rate
- Increased respiration rate

As with yellow and orange, an increase in metabolism ultimately results in an increase in appetite.

This is why warm colours are used as the main hue in most restaurants (if you want to know more about warm colours, click [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">here</span>).](/color-temperature/)

## **Lust**

In addition, desire and romance are often linked to the hue red.

The film industry uses it to establish contrast and convey the characters' sensuality.

In fact, a study found that most men find a woman more attractive when she is wearing red clothing than when she is wearing clothing of another hue.

![An image of a woman in a red dress](/assets/images/2023/07/woman-in-a-red-dress.jpg)

## **Power**

In women's culture, red symbolises power. For this reason the red tie is a symbol of authority, and that's why the stereotype of the red tie for businessmen is so popular.

![Red dress 320x214](/assets/images/2023/07/red-dress-320x214.jpg)

## **Connection with danger**

Our body's powerful reaction links directly to the feeling of danger. This is why most of the signals of danger are mainly red. Then the colour red resembles la couleur du sang, reinforcing our perception of hazard.

![Red symbol of danger 320x400](/assets/images/2023/07/Red-symbol-of-danger-320x400.jpg)
