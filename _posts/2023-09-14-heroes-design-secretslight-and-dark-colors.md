## **Black and red: passionate heroes**

Black and red are the hues of dark passion. These colours are used by villains and heroes, but they have different meanings. Villains like [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">Carnage</span>](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Carnage_(Symbiote)_(Earth-616)) or [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">Harley Quinn</span>](https://dcextendeduniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Harley_Quinn) represent passionate feelings.

Heroes, instead, tend to be represented with these colours to create a hero on the brink of evilness. A great example of this is [Deadpool](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Wade_Wilson_(Earth-616)).

The other [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">primary</span>](/color-theory2-color-wheel/) colours usually don't mix with [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">achromatic</span>](/special-color/) ones. Blue normally shifts to a powerful purple. Yellow, instead, is too cheerful to use, as we already talked about [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">here</span>](/color-costumes/).

## **White, grey and black**

White and black normally represent a connection with death ([<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">black</span>](/color-black/)) purified by the light of white. A great example of this concept is [<span class="mark has-inline-color has-vivid-purple-color" style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)">Solomon Grundy</span>,](https://batman.fandom.com/wiki/Solomon_Grundy) who is a living zombie.

Although black and white can be used to represent a moral death. For example, consider the transformation of Spider-Man into his black alter ego.

In the end, black and white tend to represent Ying and Yang, or more generally, the interaction between good and evil in characters.
