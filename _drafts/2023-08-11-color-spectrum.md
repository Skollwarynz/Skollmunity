The color spectrum of the rings in the DC universe is deeply connected with the effect of color on our minds. In this article, we'll try to explore this world together.

*[Image: The complete color spectrum of the different lantern corps in DC.]*

## **Color spectrum**

- Green:willpower
- Red:rage
- Yellow:fear
- Blue:hope
- Indigo:compassion
- Violet:love
- black:death
- white:all the emotion spectrum
- Ultraviolet: Invisible emotional spectrum
- Gold:happiness
- Orange:greed

## **Black corp**

In comics, the emotional spectrum hide is dark side too:the black ring. As all ring the ring have a source of power generated from an aspect of life or maybe not in this case. The source of this power is death. As we said in the article about black (click [here](/color-black/) to read it), this dark hue is a symbol of death itself, so in this case the correlation is pretty predictable.

This corp has the power to revive corpses and use them in an army created just to destroy life.

## **White corp**

The opposite corp is the white corp. The Ying and Yang symbolism is clear in the opposition of these two corps.

## **Green corps**

The Green Corps uses the power of hope as its source of power. In our article about green (click [here](/color-green/) to read it), we didn't talk about a correlation between green and hope. In reality, hues have different effects based on the specific shade of the same tint.Green and hope are commonly connected as symbols, especially in European culture.
