# Copyright, Fair Use & Image Attribution Policy

**Last Updated:** October 2026

## 1. Non-Commercial & Educational Purpose

**Skollmunity** is an independent, non-commercial, open source research initiative dedicated to the study of:
- Visual perception and color psychology
- Narrative visual theory in cinema, television, and fine arts
- Costume and character design in comic books and animation
- Comparative art history and design engineering

The materials, analyses, and critiques published on this platform are intended for **educational, scholarly, critical, and cultural discourse**. No monetary gain, advertising revenue, subscription fee, or commercial sponsorship is generated from this site. This describes the site's purpose; it does not by itself grant permission to use third-party works or guarantee that a particular use is lawful in every country.

## 2. Fair Use Doctrine (United States Code Title 17, Section 107)

Portions of copyrighted works—including cinematographic frames (e.g., *Whiplash*, *Squid Game*), television and anime clips (e.g., *Tengen Toppa Gurren Lagann*), and superhero comic illustrations (e.g., DC Comics, Marvel Comics)—are reproduced on this site under the **Fair Use** exception of United States Copyright Law ([17 U.S. Code § 107](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/107)):

1. **Purpose and character of use:** The use is exclusively transformative, scholarly, and non-profit educational analysis, providing commentary and color theory breakdown not present in the original works.
2. **Nature of the copyrighted work:** Fictional and cinematographic works released publicly.
3. **Amount and substantiality:** Only individual static still frames and small representative excerpts necessary to illustrate specific chromatic and compositional principles are used.
4. **Effect upon the potential market:** The analytical use does not substitute for the original films, series, or comic books, nor does it adversely affect the commercial market for the copyrighted properties.

## 3. European Union & Italian Law (Art. 70 L. 633/1941)

In accordance with Italian and EU intellectual property legislation (**Legge 22 aprile 1941 n. 633, Articolo 70**):

> *"Il riassunto, la citazione o la riproduzione di brani o di parti di opera e la loro comunicazione al pubblico sono liberi se effettuati per uso di critica o di discussione, nei limiti giustificati da tali fini e purché non costituiscano concorrenza all'utilizzazione economica dell'opera; se effettuati a fini di insegnamento o di ricerca scientifica l'utilizzo deve inoltre avvenire per finalità illustrative e per fini non commerciali."*

The reproduction of low-resolution still frames and graphical examples is conducted exclusively for critical discussion, artistic commentary, and scientific/educational research.

## 4. Direct Linking & Image Sourcing Policy

To prevent ambiguity, copyright neglect, and hotlinking issues, Skollmunity enforces a strict **Direct Linking & Attribution Standard**:

1. **Direct Clickable Access:** Analytical imagery embedded within our articles includes direct links where possible. Clicking an image either navigates to the cited source page or opens the local high-resolution asset hosted on this repository.
2. **Attribution Preservation:** Where images originate from creative platforms with attribution requirements (such as Creative Commons CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, or Freepik Free Licenses), source credit and license links are preserved.
3. **Public Domain & AI Content:** Public-domain status is relied on only when the specific work and source have been checked. Custom diagrams and renders created by Skollwarynz may be licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); each asset should be checked against its accompanying attribution before reuse.
